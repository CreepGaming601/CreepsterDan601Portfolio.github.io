About

Click to learn more about me!

To put it simply, im someone who can do things when they really enjoy it! And i am also someone who aims to make others smile through my own humor and likes. Regardless of if it sounds cringe or looks childish, theres always someone who enjoys! Another note, if you want to get updates on my projects or what i do, i recommend checking my YouTube channel or X (formerly twitter) account as im more acitve there!

My goal is to make others smile whenever they watch my videos, regardless of if they are having the time of their life or having a rough day. I want to ensure people enjoy what i make as much as i do myself. I aim to improve on my own skills and make not only myself happy but those who watch my animations happy as well!

Blender & Capcut primarily... nothing else to be honest :P

My philosophy is pretty simple. If you yourself dont enjoy what you create, then 99.9% of the time, others wouldn't enjoy it as well.
